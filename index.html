刘祎铭|Yiming Liu

Researcher @ Tencent Hunyuan.

letusgo126@gmail.com / github.com/LYMDLUT / OpenReview / CV

I am a Researcher at Tencent Hunyuan, where we work on large model evaluation. Prior to this, I worked on object detection, diffusion models, and RL infrastructure.

I received my M.Eng. in Computer Vision from Sun Yat-sen University in 2025, advised by Prof. Liang Lin at the HCP Lab, and my B.Eng. in Electronic and Information Engineering from Dalian University of Technology in 2022.

Timeline

  1. Hunyuan Jul 2025 – now

    Large Model Evaluation

    Tencent Hunyuan

    We work on evaluating large language and multimodal models at Tencent Hunyuan, including benchmarks such as VisualNeedle, EternalMath, and DramaChain Bench, as well as automated scoring and attribution pipelines for image and video generation (Q-Save).

    VisualNeedleQ-SaveDramaChain BenchEternalMath
  2. OpenRLHF Sep 2024 – now

    RL Infrastructure & KL Regularization

    OpenRLHF

    Developed KL-loss algorithms and distributed training backends (DeepSpeed AutoTP, FSDP2 + CP + TP) in OpenRLHF. Identified the gradient bias of using the k3 estimator as a loss in GRPO and proposed the unbiased k2 loss (cited by REINFORCE++), formalized in Rethinking KL Regularization in RLHF.

    OpenRLHFEMNLP 2025 paperRethinking KL Regularization
  3. ICLR 2025 Mar 2023 – Apr 2025

    Diffusion-Based Adversarial Purification

    HCP Lab, Sun Yat-sen University

    Showed from a stochastic perspective that diffusion-based purification defends through randomness rather than KL contraction or Gaussian noise masking, while its local loss landscape remains non-flat. Proposed diffusion denoising adversarial training to smooth the loss landscape across DDPM, DDIM, VP-SDE, and EDM.

    OpenReviewarXiv
  4. MMDetection Jul 2022 – Mar 2023

    Detection Transformers in MMDetection

    Shanghai AI Laboratory

    Core contributor to the Detection Transformer (refactor-detr) refactor across MMDetection 2.x and 3.x. Simplified the registry and inheritance hierarchy, unified module data structures, re-implemented Conditional DETR, DAB-DETR, and DINO with strict metric alignment, and presented the work at an OpenMMLab community talk.

    refactor-detr
